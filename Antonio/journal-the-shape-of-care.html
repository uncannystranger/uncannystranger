Frame Notes
People & connection · From the work of Abdullahi Mohamud / Uncanny Stranger

A smile, a shared space, a person beside a window: small gestures can hold the centre of a photograph.

Holding What Remains

A woman and a smiling child face one another. Their expressions and hands draw the eye before the surrounding room does. The black-and-white image places attention on the relationship within the frame, without needing a larger story to explain it.

Together, differently

Elsewhere in the collection, two people stand near bright windows and a group looks upward from a stairwell. These are different compositions, but each asks the viewer to notice how people occupy space together.

The human scale

Care, memory and connection are central to the artist biography. Read beside these portraits, those ideas become a way of looking: attend to a gesture, allow an expression to remain open, and resist reducing a person to a symbol. The images can be intimate without claiming to tell everything about the lives they show.
