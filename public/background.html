Abdullahi Mohamud

Identity

Abdullahi Mohamud, professionally known as uncannystranger, is a Somali photographer, digital artist, creative technologist, and information technology graduate based in Mogadishu, Somalia. His work focuses on visual documentation, urban environments, youth culture, and the intersection of technology and creative expression.

Early Life and Background

Abdullahi Mohamud was born on 12 December 2000 in Mogadishu, Somalia. During his early childhood, he later moved to Italy, where he spent several formative years. Growing up in a European environment exposed him to diverse cultures, visual media, and early digital technologies, contributing to his awareness of storytelling, design, and structured systems.

In 2015, he returned to Somalia and resettled in Mogadishu. The experience of moving between cultural contexts — from Somalia to Europe and back — became a defining influence on his worldview, shaping his sensitivity to environment, identity, and urban life. This dual exposure continues to inform both his creative and technical work.

Education

Abdullahi completed his secondary education at Hamar Boarding School, where he developed academic discipline, independence, and leadership skills.

He later earned a Bachelor’s Degree in Information Technology from City University of Mogadishu. His academic training covered software development, systems architecture, databases, cybersecurity fundamentals, and modern web technologies.

In December 2024, he successfully defended his undergraduate capstone thesis titled “Phishing Attack Awareness Among Somali Undergraduate Students.” The full study is publicly available on ResearchGate . He was selected by his classmates to present independently during the defense session, demonstrating clarity, confidence, and leadership in academic communication.

Creative Practice

Abdullahi’s photography emphasizes stillness, atmosphere, and observational restraint. His work focuses on natural light, transitional moments, and everyday urban scenes, particularly within Mogadishu. Rather than spectacle, his images prioritize context, presence, and quiet visual narratives.

He has over four years of experience in photography and extensive experience in photo editing and visual composition.

Origin of the Name “Uncanny Stranger”

The creative name uncannystranger emerged in 2020, the same year Abdullahi began his journey into photography. Before photography, he was deeply influenced by narrative storytelling through film and television, particularly Korean dramas.

The first Korean drama that had a lasting impact on him was Goblin.

Later in 2020, the series The Uncanny Counter resonated strongly with him.

The name Uncanny Stranger came to symbolize both the influence of this series and his identity as an observer in the photography world.
