Abdullahi Maxamed

Identity

Abdullahi Maxamed, professionally known as uncannystranger, is a Somali photographer, digital artist, and creative technologist based in Mogadishu, Somalia. His work focuses on visual documentation, urban life, youth culture, and the intersection of technology and creative expression.

Early Life

Abdullahi Maxamed was born on 12 December 2000 in Mogadishu, Somalia. Growing up in the capital city, he developed an early awareness of how cities carry memory through architecture, daily routines, and human presence.

Education

He completed his secondary education at Hamar Boarding School, where he developed academic discipline, independence, and leadership skills.

He is an alumnus of City University of Mogadishu, where he earned a Bachelor of Information Technology. His academic training covered software development, systems thinking, cybersecurity awareness, and modern web technologies.

In December 2024, he successfully defended his undergraduate capstone thesis titled “Phishing Attack Awareness Among Somali Undergraduate Students.” He was selected by his class to present independently during the defense session, demonstrating confidence, clarity, and leadership in academic communication.

Professional Background

In addition to his creative practice, Abdullahi has professional experience in digital marketing, software development, graphic and web design, and editorial leadership. His multidisciplinary background allows him to operate across both technical and creative domains.

Creative Practice

Abdullahi describes his photography as a study of stillness. His work emphasizes atmosphere, natural light, and transitional moments rather than spectacle, often documenting everyday life in Mogadishu with restraint and intention.

Origin of the Name “Uncanny Stranger”

The name Uncanny Stranger emerged during the same period Abdullahi began his journey into photography. Prior to photography, he was deeply influenced by narrative storytelling through film and television, particularly Korean dramas. One of the earliest series that left a lasting impression on him was Goblin, which led him to explore many other Korean dramas and develop an appreciation for atmosphere, pacing, and emotional depth.

In 2020, he watched The Uncanny Counter, a series he connected with strongly on a personal level. The word “uncanny” resonated with the feeling of existing between worlds — observing quietly from the edges. When forming a creative identity, he chose to combine “uncanny” with “stranger” to reflect his position at the time: new to photography, learning, observing, and documenting without intrusion.

The name Uncanny Stranger came to represent entering the photography world as an outsider — attentive, reflective, and intentionally distant — a perspective that continues to shape his visual approach today.
