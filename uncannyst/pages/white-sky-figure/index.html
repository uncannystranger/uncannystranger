White Sky Figure

13 Apr, 2026 Journal, Photo

A figure stands inside a bright hush, almost erased and still unmistakably present.

High light can make the world feel unfinished. Edges loosen, details surrender, and what remains is the shape of attention itself.

This frame lives in that exposure. It lets the face and body hover near disappearance, then pulls them back with the force of a glance.

The result is not absence. It is presence made fragile enough to notice.
